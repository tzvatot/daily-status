- Diagnosed and resolved a four-layer TLS/OAuth connectivity failure between the fulfillment controller, Authorino, and Keycloak on vmaas-dev. Root causes included a missing CA certificate in the shared CA bundle, newly required identity provider flags in the latest controller image, a volume mount collision introduced by the Authorino operator, and a CA trust path issue after relocating the mount. All four layers are now resolved — the controller is running and reconciling, and both demo clusters progressed to provisioning.
