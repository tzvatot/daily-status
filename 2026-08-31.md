# Daily Status - 2026-08-31

## Accomplishments
- Progressed work on [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) (part of [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604)) to make the reported progress status more accurate by deriving its detail from the furthest-advanced stage of cluster provisioning, so users see a meaningful indication of where their cluster is in the setup process.
- Settled on an order-independent approach that ranks provisioning stages by a fixed priority rather than list order, improving reliability of the reported status.
- Identified a data consistency issue where a duplicate untyped condition should be replaced by its typed equivalent, and planned the fix.
- Flagged a design documentation refinement to keep the design doc at an appropriate level of abstraction, leaving specific status wording to the implementation.

## Risks & Challenges
None.

## Key Effort
Improving the accuracy and reliability of cluster provisioning progress status reporting in the operator.
