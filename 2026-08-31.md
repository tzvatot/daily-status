# Daily Status - 2026-08-31

## Accomplishments
- Progressed work on [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) (part of [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604)) to give the PROGRESSING status a more meaningful reason and human-readable message derived from the furthest-advanced stage of cluster provisioning, so users get clearer, order-independent feedback on where their cluster stands.
- Finalized the design approach for how progress reasons and messages are surfaced, keeping the status boolean single-sourced while enriching the accompanying detail.
- Identified a fix to remove a duplicate condition and rely on the correctly typed storage-readiness condition instead.

## Risks & Challenges
- Implementation is still in progress: the reason/message overlay is not yet coded, validation and self-review have not been run, and the draft has not been published for review.
- Follow-up design cleanup is needed on the [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604) design doc to remove overly implementation-specific vocabulary, keeping the design at the conceptual level and letting the implementation own the exact tokens.

## Key Effort
Enhancing the PROGRESSING status feedback so users receive an accurate, human-readable indication of their cluster's current provisioning stage.
