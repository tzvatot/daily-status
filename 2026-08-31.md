# Daily Status - 2026-08-31

## Accomplishments
- Progressed work on deriving richer status feedback for cluster provisioning, so that the in-progress state reflects the furthest-advanced provisioning sub-stage (e.g. control plane readiness) in an order-independent way, giving users clearer visibility into where their cluster is in the process ([OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441), part of [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604)).
- Finalized the design decision that the human-readable status reason and message should be driven by the current sub-stage condition, keeping the overall progress signal single-sourced.
- Identified and scoped a fix to remove a duplicate storage-readiness condition in favor of the strongly-typed equivalent.
- Coding of the sub-stage reason/message overlay is in progress; validation, self-review, and publishing of the draft remain outstanding.
- Noted a follow-up design cleanup for [OSAC-1604](https://github.com/osac-project/osac-operator/pull/227) to keep the design document at the conceptual level rather than dictating specific implementation-owned status tokens.

## Risks & Challenges
None.

## Key Effort
Improving the in-progress status feedback for cluster provisioning so users can see the furthest-advanced sub-stage of their cluster.
