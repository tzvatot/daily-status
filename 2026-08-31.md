# Daily Status - 2026-08-31

## Accomplishments
- Progressed work on [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) (part of [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604)) to give the PROGRESSING status a more meaningful reason and human-readable message derived from the furthest-advanced stage of cluster provisioning, so users get clearer, order-independent visibility into where their cluster is in the lifecycle.
- Finalized the design decision to source the progressing reason and message from the most-advanced sub-stage while keeping the overall progressing indicator single-sourced, ensuring consistent and predictable status reporting.
- Identified a fix to eliminate a duplicate storage-readiness condition in favor of the typed variant, improving correctness of the status conditions.
- Coding of the reason/message overlay is still in progress, with validation, self-review, and PR publishing remaining before the change is ready for review.

## Risks & Challenges
- The design documentation for [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604) currently prescribes overly implementation-specific vocabulary for the progressing reason; this needs to be walked back so the design stays high-level and leaves exact wording to the implementation.

## Key Effort
Improving the clarity and accuracy of cluster provisioning progress reporting for [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441).
