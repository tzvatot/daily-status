# Daily Status - 2026-10-29

- Addressed four outstanding review findings in the Netris physical attachment work, improving diagnostic safety, attachment ownership, DHCP retry behavior, and malformed-URL handling; added regression coverage and documented safe legacy snapshot migration.
- Deduplicated attachment network identity resolution and reservation lifecycles in response to review feedback.
- Validated the changes through unit and role regressions, idempotency and integration scenarios, formatting, scoped linting, pre-commit checks, and secret scans.
