# Daily Status - 2026-10-29

- Pushed the fixes to the draft PR, where CI is running on the new head.

## Risks & Challenges
- The shared provisioning-network concurrency concern remains unverified. There is no failing unit test or reproduced live-provider failure, and existing tenant-network locking does not yet establish that deployment supplies and acquires a shared provisioning lock or that stale-write behavior occurs with real Netris.
- The earlier characterization of this as a confirmed bug was too strong and has been corrected. The two-host stale-write scenario must be reproduced or dismissed against real Netris before reconsidering [OSAC-4923](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4923).
- Remaining work includes an interleaved two-host regression and real-provider concurrency evidence. Any fix must respect the accepted backend-atomic and conflict-detection requirements; changing the coordination model or declaring physical mutations unsupported would require an explicit design and support decision.
- The PR remains draft with CI in progress, and the Jira ticket remains open.
