# Daily Status - 2026-10-29

## Accomplishments
- Investigated the Slack review feedback on the Netris attachment work and reproduced four outstanding issues ([OSAC-6754](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-6754)).
- Implemented fixes for diagnostic redaction, stable attachment ownership, DHCP retry policy, and malformed-URL handling, then added regression tests and documented safe legacy snapshot migration.
- Reworked attachment V-Net identity resolution and ExternalIP/cluster-ingress reservation lifecycles to remove duplication identified during review.
- Validated the changes with a broad test pass covering unit tests, supported Ansible versions, Kind idempotency scenarios, and integration paths, along with formatting, lint, pre-commit hooks, and secret scans.
- Pushed the fixes to the open PR for review ([osac-project/osac#2049](https://github.com/osac-project/osac/pull/2049)); the PR remains in draft and CI is running on the new head.

## Risks & Challenges
- A shared provisioning V-Net lost-update race in Netris physical attachments can still restore another host's ports and erase recovery state; concurrency-safe provider updates are not yet verified.
- The remaining blocker requires backend-atomic or conditional updates because the accepted contract forbids manager locks.
- An interleaved two-host safety regression and real-provider concurrency validation are still outstanding.
- Marking physical mutations unsupported or fail-closed requires an explicit support decision, since it could also block cleanup.

## Key Effort
Review-driven hardening of the Netris attachment implementation, focused on correctness, safe migration, and resolving the remaining concurrency blocker.
