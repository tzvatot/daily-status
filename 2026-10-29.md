Tenant-network locking exists, but shared provisioning lease acquisition and actual stale-write behavior against Netris still need verification before this can be treated as a confirmed bug or [OSAC-4923](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4923) reopened.
- Remaining work includes adding an interleaved two-host regression and real-provider concurrency evidence; any confirmed fix must respect the accepted contract's backend-atomic/conflict-detection requirement, and adding manager locks or marking physical mutations unsupported requires an explicit design decision.
