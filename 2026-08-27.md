# Daily Status - 2026-08-27

## Accomplishments
- Responded to review feedback on the [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604) granular cluster status reporting design ([enhancement-proposals#227](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/227)), renaming the cluster-level rollup status to align with OpenShift and HyperShift conventions and clarifying ambiguity that had confused reviewers; posted replies and updated the design document, now awaiting re-review and approval.
- Diagnosed a broken container image build under [OSAC-4010](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4010) caused by an earlier removal of a directory the build still referenced, confirmed no upstream fix exists, identified a fix approach, and requested an ETA from the ticket assignee before proceeding.
- Re-reviewed the Catalog Items v2 typed provisioning governance design ([OSAC-3538](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-3538), [enhancement-proposals#228](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/228)), verifying which prior findings were resolved or accepted, flagging still-open governance semantics and a disputed example; review drafted but not yet posted pending a decision on the review verdict.
- Fixed an order-dependence defect in cluster status condition mapping under [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) (a story of [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604)) so that status is derived deterministically regardless of condition ordering, added guard tests to prevent future drift, and validated the build and full test suite; code committed and pushed to a fork but publishing is paused at a self-review gate over two open design concerns around how progressing status is derived and messaged.
