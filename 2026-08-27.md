# Daily Status - 2026-08-27

## Accomplishments
- Responded to review feedback on the [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604) granular cluster status reporting design ([enhancement-proposals#227](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/227)), renaming the cluster-level rollup condition to align with established OpenShift and HyperShift conventions and clarifying the status-reporting contract; updated the design document and posted replies, now awaiting re-review and approval.
- Diagnosed a broken container image build ([OSAC-4010](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4010)) caused by a prior cleanup effort removing a required dependency; identified a fix and reached out to the ticket assignee for an ETA before proceeding.
- Re-reviewed the Catalog Items v2 typed provisioning governance design ([OSAC-3538](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-3538), [enhancement-proposals#228](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/228)), confirming resolution of prior findings and flagging remaining open concerns around governance semantics and scope; review drafted but not yet posted pending a decision.
- Implemented an order-independent mapping for cluster status feedback conditions ([OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441)), fixing a bug where progress status could be incorrectly overwritten, and added safeguards and tests to prevent future drift; all validation passed and changes pushed to a fork, but the PR is paused at a self-review gate pending resolution of an open design question about how progress status should behave over a cluster's lifecycle.

## Risks & Challenges
- The container image build and development environment are broken on the upstream main branch until the [OSAC-4010](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4010) fix lands.
- The [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) PR is blocked at a review gate by an unresolved design question about whether an available cluster should re-enter a progressing state when a dependent condition later fails; this must be verified against the [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604) design before proceeding.
- The [OSAC-4010](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4010) fix is on hold awaiting an ETA response from the ticket assignee.

## Key Effort
Advancing the granular cluster status reporting initiative through design review responses and implementation of order-independent status condition mapping.
