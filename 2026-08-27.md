# Daily Status - 2026-08-27

## Accomplishments
- Responded to review feedback on the [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604) granular cluster status reporting design ([enhancement-proposals#227](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/227)), renaming the cluster-level readiness rollup condition to align with established OpenShift, HyperShift, and Kubernetes conventions and resolve reviewer confusion; posted replies and pushed the updated design, now awaiting re-review and approval.
- Diagnosed why the container image build is broken on upstream main under [OSAC-4010](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4010): a prior cleanup effort removed a source script still referenced by the build, and confirmed no upstream fix has merged; identified a fix approach but held off implementing it pending an ETA from the ticket assignee.
- Re-reviewed the [OSAC-3538](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-3538) Catalog Items v2 typed provisioning governance design ([enhancement-proposals#228](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/228)), confirming which prior findings were fixed or accepted, flagging remaining open concerns around governance semantics and scope; review drafted but not yet posted pending user decision.
- Implemented an order-independent fix for the cluster status feedback mapping under [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) (first story of [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604)), eliminating a bug where progress status could be overwritten depending on processing order, and added guardrail tests; code is written, validated, and pushed to a fork, but publishing is paused at a self-review gate pending two design resolutions.

## Risks & Challenges
- The local development environment (container image build and dev shell entry) is broken on upstream main until the [OSAC-4010](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4010) fix lands, and that fix is currently blocked awaiting an ETA from the ticket assignee.
- Publishing the [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) work is paused pending resolution of two design questions: a naming/consistency issue between how conditions are read and written, and a broader open question of whether progress status should be re-entered after a cluster becomes available but a dependency later degrades — the latter must be verified against the [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604) design before deciding.

## Key Effort
Advancing the granular cluster status reporting initiative through design review response and implementation of order-independent status mapping, while unblocking the broken development environment.
