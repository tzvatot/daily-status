# Daily Status - 2026-08-27

## Accomplishments
- Responded to design review on granular cluster status reporting ([OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604), [enhancement-proposals#227](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/227)) by renaming the cluster-level rollup status to align with established OpenShift, HyperShift, and Kubernetes conventions, resolving reviewer confusion; updated the design document and posted replies, now awaiting re-review and approval.

- Re-reviewed the Catalog Items v2 typed provisioning governance design ([OSAC-3538](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-3538), [enhancement-proposals#228](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/228)), confirming which prior findings were fixed or accepted and flagging remaining open concerns around governance semantics and an example inconsistency; review drafted but not yet posted pending user decision.
