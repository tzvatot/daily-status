# Daily Status - 2026-08-27

## Accomplishments
- Responded to review feedback on the [Granular Cluster Status Reporting design proposal](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/227) for [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604), including renaming the cluster-level rollup status to align with established OpenShift and HyperShift conventions and clarifying status-vs-condition semantics; updated the design document and posted replies, now awaiting re-review and approval.
- Diagnosed a broken container image build under [OSAC-4010](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4010) caused by a prior cleanup effort removing a required source file; confirmed no upstream fix exists yet, identified a viable remediation, and requested an ETA from the ticket assignee before proceeding.
- Re-reviewed the [Catalog Items v2 typed provisioning governance design](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/228) for [OSAC-3538](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-3538), verifying which prior findings were resolved or accepted, flagging remaining open concerns, and drafting a review for user decision before posting.
- Implemented an order-independent status condition mapping for [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) to fix a bug where progress status could be incorrectly overwritten depending on condition order; added guard tests, validated the full test suite, and pushed the branch, but paused publishing at a self-review gate pending resolution of open design questions.

## Risks & Challenges
- The container image build and containerized development environment are broken on upstream main until [OSAC-4010](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4010) is resolved, and the fix is currently blocked awaiting an ETA from the assignee.
- The [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) work is paused at review, with two outstanding design issues: a naming inconsistency between condition readers and writers, and an unresolved question of whether the progress status should behave as a state machine when a cluster that is already available later degrades — this must be verified against the [OSAC-1604 design](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/227) before finalizing.

## Key Effort
Advancing granular cluster status reporting for [OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604) through design review response and implementation of order-independent status condition mapping.
