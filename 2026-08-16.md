# Daily Status - 2026-08-16

## Accomplishments
- Advanced the product requirements for granular cluster status reporting ([OSAC-1604](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-1604)), refining the draft proposal ([enhancement-proposals#209](https://github.com/osac-project/enhancement-proposals/pull/209)) to give users clear, meaningful visibility into where their cluster is in its lifecycle.
- Defined user-facing acceptance criteria around named lifecycle stages so status is expressed in terms users can understand rather than internal implementation concepts.
- Added non-functional requirements covering data freshness, consistency across interfaces, and avoiding regressions, along with supporting assumptions to ground the proposed stages.
- Reframed the problem statement, scope, and dependencies to focus on user-observable behavior, removing internal design details that had leaked into the requirements.
- Clarified the target personas and confirmed that status reporting is consistent across them, differing only in scope.
- Researched upstream cluster and node lifecycle conditions to ground the proposed stage set and correct outdated figures in the draft.
- Verified the proposal against the standard template and existing merged proposals, ensuring all validation checks pass, and pushed the updates for review.

## Risks & Challenges
None.

Finalizing the product requirements for granular cluster status reporting to deliver clearer cluster lifecycle visibility to users.
