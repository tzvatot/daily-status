# Daily Status - 2026-11-11

## Accomplishments
- Implemented the core conformance suite for [OSAC-7454](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-7454), including suite execution, configuration and selection, verdict and reporting, diagnostics, and operability. Validation passed, and draft [osac#2340](https://github.com/osac-project/osac/pull/2340) is open for review.
- Triaged the Netris CA regression for [OSAC-7635](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-7635), traced it to [osac#2051](https://github.com/osac-project/osac/pull/2051), and confirmed the corrective change landed on main through [osac#2151](https://github.com/osac-project/osac/pull/2151). Established that the 0.4 release candidates remain affected and closed the ticket as post-0.4.
- Built and lab-validated a release-0.4 backport of the Netris CA fix for [OSAC-7635](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-7635), then opened draft [osac#2383](https://github.com/osac-project/osac/pull/2383). Validation confirmed successful networking with certificate validation enabled, and lab resources and configuration were restored afterward. The PR remains draft pending review.

## Risks & Challenges
- The broader osac unit suite still has 16 unrelated pre-existing failures, so it does not yet provide a clean project-wide signal.
- The 0.4 release candidates remain affected by the Netris CA issue, and the release-0.4 backport is still awaiting review.

## Key Effort
Primary focus was delivering the conformance suite core while triaging and backporting the Netris CA regression for the 0.4 release line.
