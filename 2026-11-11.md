- Implemented the core OSAC conformance suite to support configurable test selection, verdicts, reporting, and operability for validation workflows; submitted [osac#2340](https://github.com/osac-project/osac/pull/2340) as a draft PR for review under [OSAC-7454](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-7454).
