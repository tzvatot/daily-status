This is the same class of defect [OSAC-4441](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OSAC-4441) and [osac#1299](https://github.com/osac-project/osac/pull/1299) are meant to address, suggesting the current fix may be incomplete — investigation of operator logs is still outstanding.
- The automation-template changes that unblocked provisioning are local-only workarounds on the test blade and are not committed, so the environment is not reproducible from source.
- The nested-virtualization test environment remains fragile, with repeated manual intervention needed for boot order, time sync, and leftover resources from failed runs.
